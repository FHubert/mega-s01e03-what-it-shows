The two problems

  1. parser. The shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first, so any day of 12 or lower swaps with the month. It reaches the parser by app, by email and through the booking-v2 form. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale. Fixed in fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  2. hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The symptom is the same swap, but the parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so the hint is gone with it.

How the split is made

  • Rows come from reports-4471.json. The export pages overlap, so a report counts once per id.
  • The cause comes from the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run (causes.ts).
  • What a report was tagged or read as does not make a problem. Intake tags from the reporter’s browser, and the desk’s notes (desk-notes.json) record how a report read. Both are shown above, and both count under the cause.
  • The numbers above are computed at render time, so they follow the data as the desk adds reports.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line does not make a matching cause (decision 0001). Before you close a big ticket as one issue, group its reports by cause.
  • Count by cause, not by tag or by reading. A tag is a guess; a reading is a symptom.
