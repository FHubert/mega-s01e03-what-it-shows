The queued parser fix closes only the first row below. The other two keep coming after it ships.

The three problems

  1. parser. The shared parser reads dates month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first, so any day of 12 or lower swaps with the month. It reaches the parser by app, email and the booking-v2 form.
  2. hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The symptom is the same swap, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to change.
  3. one-day. Some bookings move by one day, not by a swapped month: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but #4471 still footnoted them under the parser. This is a time zone bug.

How the split is made

  • Rows come from reports-4471.json. The export pages overlap, so a report is counted once per id.
  • Cause comes from the tenant and the channel (causes.ts), not from the intake tag. Intake guesses from the browser and disagrees with the tenant often.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) counts under one-day.
  • The numbers above are computed at render time, so they follow the data as the desk adds reports.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line does not make a matching cause (decision 0001). Before you close a big ticket as one issue, read the report bodies and group them by cause.
  • A footnote is not a ticket. If the desk reads reports differently, give them a row of their own.
